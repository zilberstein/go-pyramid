Rules

Each player builds a pyramid: the base is A 2 3 4, then 5 6 7, then 8 9, and 10 on top. A card can only be placed once both cards supporting it are in place (5 needs A and 2, 6 needs 2 and 3, 8 needs 5 and 6, 10 needs 8 and 9). Base cards can be placed any time. Face cards are worthless.

Each turn you either place and/or discard any cards from your hand, or swap your whole hand with the 5 face-down cards in the middle. After both players have moved, hands are refilled to 5 cards. When the deck runs out, the discard pile is shuffled in.

The first player to complete their pyramid at the end of a round wins. If both complete it in the same round, it's a draw.
